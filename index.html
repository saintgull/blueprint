Why This Matters

Great community projects fail at alarming rates. Without shared knowledge, grassroots initiatives face high risk, wasted capital, and lost potential for self-governance. Every failed project represents lost resources and missed opportunities for community resilience.

30%+
Target Success Rate Increase

What We're Building

Knowledge infrastructure for community action. We collect, systematize, and share the methodologies behind grassroots projects—turning isolated attempts into collective wisdom. This enables:

  • Informed decision-making for new initiatives
  • Risk reduction through learning from others
  • Faster iteration and adaptation
  • Network governance through shared knowledge

How We're Doing It

Our multidisciplinary team combines professional project management (PMI), grassroots community work, and academic research to translate experience into scalable knowledge.
